## IEX

Market Data & Connectivity:
https://www.iex.io/products/equities/market-data-connectivity

https://iextrading.com/trading/market-data/

HIST terms:
https://www.iex.io/legal/hist-data-terms

Market-data resources/specifications:
https://www.iex.io/resources/trading/market-data

Verified for this pack:
- HIST is free and T+1.
- Current product page says the most recent twelve months are available.
- TOPS provides aggregated top-of-book quotations and last-sale data.
- DEEP+ provides an order-by-order view of resting displayed orders.
- IEX data is venue-specific.
- Current official download page shows recent daily files can be very large.
